Tarna Klitzner

TKLA — Tarna Klitzner Landscape Architects · SOUTH AFRICA

Tarna Klitzner founded TKLA in Cape Town in 1995, following an architecture degree at the University of Cape Town and a master's in landscape architecture at the University of Pennsylvania. Her practice spans public and private commissions across southern Africa, with an emphasis on human-centred design, community engagement, and the interplay between urban and natural environments. She is a full-time professor in the Department of Landscape Architecture at UCT.
