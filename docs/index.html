13 landscape architects. One ancient format. Infinite interpretations. In 1926, architect Nicolau M. Rubió i Tudurí, ceramicist Llorenç Artigas and painter Raoul Dufy created the jardinets de saló — miniature ceramic worlds small enough to rest on a table, big enough to hold a landscape. A century later, thirteen of the world's leading landscape architects were invited to reinvent them. An exhibition you walk through, scattered across the Jardí Botànic de Barcelona — waiting to be found. Parlour Gardens · Barcelona, June to October 2026 ✕
