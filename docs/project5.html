Making of Harris' project

Phil Harris

Troppo Architects · AUSTRALIA

Phil Harris is a founding director of Troppo Architects, whose practice has operated for over three decades across Australia's most remote landscapes — the Northern Territory, the Kimberley, the APY Lands and Cape York. Harris and co-founder Adrian Welke received the 2014 AIA Gold Medal and the Global Sustainable Architecture Award (Paris, 2010), the only Australian practice to receive that honour. The practice has won over 40 Australian Institute of Architects awards.
