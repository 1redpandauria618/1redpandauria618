Adam Greenspan

PWP Landscape Architecture · USA

Adam Greenspan is a design partner at PWP Landscape Architecture, San Francisco. Recent projects include the Newport Beach Civic Center Park, Constitution Gardens on the National Mall in Washington DC, the Transbay Transit Center Park and Marina Bay Sands in Singapore. His background in studio art and sociology informs a design approach rooted in collaboration with communities, artists, and ecologists. He is a Fellow of the American Society of Landscape Architects.
