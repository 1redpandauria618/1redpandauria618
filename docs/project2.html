Catherine Mosbach

Mosbach Paysagistes · FRANCE

Catherine Mosbach leads Mosbach Paysagistes, a Paris-based practice with extensive experience in urban landscapes of high patrimonial and environmental value. The studio won the Équerre d'Argent alongside SANAA for the Louvre Lens Museum Park and the Iconic Concept Award from the German Design Council. Named Firm of the Year 2021 (Architecture Master Prize, Los Angeles), recent projects include commissions for the Triennale Milano (2023) and Harvard GSD (2024).
