Special Acknowledgement

The photographs of the jardí de saló painted by Nicolau M. Rubió i Tudurí and produced by Ceràmiques Serra are reproduced with the kind permission of the Serra family. This piece — completed by Rubió i Tudurí in the final months of his life, at the request of the 1979 Fundació Joan Miró exhibition — stands as his last creative work. Special thanks to Josep Ramon Reventós for his first-hand insights and for sharing images of original models from his personal collection. Finally, deep felt thank you Marta Cervelló and Montse Rivero for their historical assessment of the original ceramic models by Artigas, Rubió i Tudurí and Dufy.
